Our Friendship Story 📖

A little story about ordinary school days that became extraordinary memories.

🌱

When our friendship began

It started with simple conversations, sitting together, sharing jokes and slowly becoming a group that made school feel special.

❄️

The winter rooftop memory

That peaceful winter day on the school rooftop: all of us together, laughing, teasing one another and forgetting the worries of the day.

📚

The assignment I'll never forget

When I had not completed the assignment, Saim shared the work he had written. When the teacher got upset, he even asked that I should not be punished. That kindness is still precious to me.

😂

Our silly jokes

Sometimes the smallest jokes made the biggest laughs. Even a normal school day could become a memory because we were together.

Memories that travel with us

Schools and routines can change, but the good moments we shared remain part of our story.
